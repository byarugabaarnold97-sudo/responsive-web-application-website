Pipe Installation

John has extensive expertise in pipe installation developed over 17 years at NWSC. He works with PVC, PPR, HDPE, and galvanized steel pipes — selecting materials based on project requirements and site conditions.

His process covers reading technical drawings, precision cutting and joining, pressure testing after installation, and coordinating with engineers on large-scale projects to ensure code compliance and long-term durability.

🔧

Leak Detection & Repair

Using pressure testing, visual inspection, and specialized electronic tracing equipment, John locates hidden leaks in residential and municipal systems — including complex underground networks — with minimal excavation.

Once located, he applies lasting repair methods and verifies results with post-repair pressure tests, reducing water loss and protecting structural integrity.

🏠

Bathroom & Kitchen Plumbing

John installs and maintains sinks, toilets, showers, bathtubs, water heaters, faucets, and drainage systems with correct pipe alignment, pressure balancing, and leak-free connections for hygiene-compliant results.

He also troubleshoots common issues including clogged drains, low pressure, and faulty fixtures — delivering reliable, long-lasting outcomes tailored to client needs.

🌧️

Drainage & Sewer Systems

Comprehensive drainage design, installation, and maintenance for residential, commercial, and municipal clients — including HDPE storm drains, gravity sewers, and interceptor chambers.

John ensures correct falls, joint sealing, and compliance with NEMA and NWSC design standards to prevent flooding, backflow, and environmental contamination.

🪣

Water Tank Installation

Supply and professional installation of plastic and concrete water storage tanks ranging from 500 to 10,000+ litres — including structural bases, inlet/outlet piping, float valves, overflow pipes, and NWSC connection permits.

John designs distribution manifolds for multi-tenant buildings and includes disinfection procedures before handover.

🌍

NWSC Mainline Repair

As a senior NWSC plumber, John has led emergency repairs on ductile iron, steel, and HDPE mains across Kampala — restoring supply to thousands of households within hours of a burst or fault event.

He applies safe-dig procedures, proper pipe-bridging techniques, and pressure testing to ensure restored mains meet long-term performance standards without repeat failures.
